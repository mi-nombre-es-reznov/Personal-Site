Problem Solution Write-Up
I was facing an issue with the accumulation of past-due tickets, especially on the state reporting side, which depends heavily on county billing and state processing timelines. Manually updating these overdue tickets to reflect realistic due dates was inefficient and time-consuming, causing workflow misalignment and a backlog of overdue tasks. To address this, I implemented an automation in the Fresh Service platform using the Workflow Automator tool. This automation extends the due dates of my overdue tickets by 30 days whenever I assign a ticket to myself. It ensures only my tickets are affected, maintaining control and preventing mass updates across the company. Additionally, it logs a note in each updated ticket to provide clarity and transparency about the change. View
