A Little About Me!

I'm Nicholas, a proud graduate of the University of California, Riverside (UCR), where I earned my Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering in 2020. My journey started at Reedley College, part of the State Center Community College District (SCCCD), where I earned Associate's degrees in Mathematics, Physics, Computer Science, and Engineering before transferring to UCR. Both schools played a big role in shaping the direction of my career and interests.

While my academic background is diverse, I focused on Embedded Systems during my time at UCR, especially for my senior design project. I found the field fascinating because of the close interaction between hardware and software. I enjoy seeing physical components and code work together to build something functional.

Unfortunately, I haven't worked directly in Embedded Systems or Computer Engineering within my employment, but each job I've had has taught me something new about my field of study. I've seen many times how my engineering background can be applied to solve real problems and improve workflows. I'm still learning and growing, and I'm excited to see where my path leads next.

One place I do get to use my engineering skills is in my personal projects. I've been working on a number of them to keep learning and experimenting with new tools and technologies. These projects let me apply what I learned in school and explore areas that interest me — including this website, which is one of those projects!

I enjoy the challenge of learning something new and applying it to real-world problems. Personal projects are one of my favorite ways to keep growing. You can check out some of them in the Portfolio section of this site.
