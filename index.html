A Little About Me!

I am Nicholas, a proud graduate from the University of California, Riverside (UCR) with a Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering. I graduated in 2020 and have been exploring life in the workforce since then.

It took a lot to get to this point and it would not be fair to leave out another college that I attended that help prepare me for my future. I also attended the State Center Community College District (SCCCD) before transferring to UCR. My Home campus was Reedley College, where I earned my four Associate's degree in Mathematics, Physics, Computer Science, and Engineering.

While my educational experience is diverse, I decided to focus more on the Embedded Systems route for my area of expertise and senior design project, while at UCR. I found this area very fascinating because of the close interaction between hardware and software. I enjoy seeing computers work harmoniously with physical components to create a functional system between hardware and software. Putting these two seemingly different worlds together is what makes Embedded Systems so interesting to me and opens up a world of possibilities for future projects.

Unfortunately, my roles since college have not been in the Embedded Systems field nor Computer Engineering, but they still play a huge role in my discovery of what exists outside of the classroom. Within each role that I have held since leaving college, I have seen the numerous possibilities that technology can bring to the table and how I could potentially be a part of it. Despite my roles not being directly related to my field of study, I can still see how my background in Computer Engineering can be used to help improve processes and workflows - even if solving problems through something like a SaaS platform or consulting services. I am still learning and growing in my career, and I am excited to see where my journey takes me next!

One place I do have control to use my Computer Engineering skills is through my personal projects. I have been working on several projects on the side to help me learn new skills and technologies. These projects allow me to apply what I have learned in school and explore new areas of interest. In fact, the website you are currently visiting is one of my personal projects!

I enjoy the challenge of learning new things and applying them to real-world problems. I believe that personal projects are a great way to learn and grow. You can see some of my projects in the Portfolio section of this website.
